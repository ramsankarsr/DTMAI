TAK-003 Clinical Efficacy & Waning Mechanics

TAK-003 (Qdenga) provides robust protective efficacy against all 4 dengue serotypes, with differential waning curves based on baseline serostatus:

  • Seropositive Individuals ($V^+$): Initial efficacy ~72% against symptomatic dengue, ~90% against hospitalization, with extended persistence.
  • Seronegative Individuals ($V^-$): Initial efficacy ~56% against symptomatic dengue, ~85% against hospitalization, with gradual waning over 4.5+ years.
  • Natural Breakthrough Boosting: Breakthrough natural infections boost antibody titers, transitioning hosts along subsequent protective curves (Episodes 1 → 2 → 3 → 4).
Vector Dynamics & Biological Suppression

Models Aedes aegypti / albopictus mosquito population dynamics with sinusoidal seasonal capacity ($p_1 = 0.341$, $p_2 = -0.512$), 14-day adult lifespan, and 10-day extrinsic incubation period:

  • Wolbachia Release: Reduces vector competence and biting transmission rate ($b$).
  • Larviciding & Source Reduction: Suppresses vector-to-host density ($V/H$).
  • Synergy with Vaccination: Dual intervention suppresses force of infection while providing individual immunological protection.

PARAMETER DEFINITIONS

⚙️ Interactive Sidebar Parameters & Configurations

💉 Vaccination Strategy Controls

  • Enable Vaccination (TAK-003): Toggles annual pulsed routine immunization for eligible age cohorts.
  • Routine Target Age (Years): Target age cohort (slider: 4–18 years). In Thailand, age 11 captures highest pre-exposure seropositivity without missing peak adolescent disease incidence.
  • Coverage Rate (%): Percentage of the target single-year birth cohort receiving the full 2-dose regimen (default: 87% national EPI target).
  • Catch-Up Campaign (Yr 1): One-time mass immunization campaign deployed in Year 1 across ages 10 to 17.
  • Pre-Screening (Test-Before-Vac): Simulates rapid pre-vaccination serological screening to restrict administration solely to seropositive individuals.

🦟 Vector Control & Health Economics Controls

  • Enable Vector Control: Modifies mosquito-to-human transmission dynamics via Wolbachia introgression or source reduction.
  • Biting Rate Reduction (α): Proportional reduction in daily biting frequency (0–100%).
  • Mosquito Density Ratio Reduction ($N_v/N_h$): Proportional reduction in adult female Aedes aegypti per person.
  • Vaccine Price ($/Dose): Unit acquisition cost per dose (default: $30/dose; 2-dose series = $60).
  • Discount Rate (%/Year): Annual economic discount rate for costs and health utilities (default: 3.0%).
  • Perspective: Societal (direct medical + non-medical + indirect productivity) vs Healthcare Payer (direct medical only).

ECONOMIC FRAMEWORK

💰 Health Economics & HEOR Decision Framework

DALYs & QALYs
Burden of Disease Metrics

$$\text{DALY} = \text{YLL} + \text{YLD}$$

Disability-Adjusted Life Years combine Years of Life Lost due to premature dengue mortality ($\text{YLL}$) with Years Lived with Disability ($\text{YLD}$), using standardized disability weights: Outpatient ($DW = 0.051$), Inpatient ($DW = 0.210$), Fatal ($DW = 1.000$).

ICER & Dominance
Cost-Effectiveness Ratio

$$\text{ICER} = \frac{\Delta \text{Cost}}{\Delta \text{DALYs Averted}}$$

If an intervention yields more health benefits ($\Delta \text{DALYs} > 0$) while resulting in net cost savings ($\Delta \text{Cost} < 0$), it is mathematically classified as DOMINANT (cost-saving and superior).

WTP & Price Ceiling
Decision Thresholds

$$\text{WTP Threshold} \approx \$7,000 / \text{DALY}$$

Represents $1\times\text{GDP per capita}$ in Thailand. The Max Threshold Price calculates the highest vaccine price per dose at which the vaccination program remains cost-effective compared to baseline.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

📐 Mathematical Transmission Architecture

Formulation of the 4-serotype age-structured host-vector ODE system ($i \in \{1,2,3,4\}$):

Human Force of Infection:
\lambda_{h,i}(t) = \beta_h \cdot b \cdot \frac{I_{v,i}(t)}{N_h(t)}
Vector Force of Infection:
\lambda_{v,i}(t) = \beta_v \cdot b \cdot \frac{I_{h,i}(t) + \gamma_{CE} \cdot I_{h,i}^{\text{sec}}(t)}{N_h(t)}
ADE & Cross-Protection:
\gamma_{CE} = 1.30 \quad (\text{ADE Infectivity Enhancer}), \quad \sigma_{CP} = \frac{1}{0.5\text{ yr}} \quad (\text{Cross-Protection Clearance})
Markov Efficacy Waning:
V_{a, s, k} \xrightarrow{\omega_k} V_{a, s, k+1} \quad (k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}) \quad \text{with natural boosting reset}
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is Routine R11 (Age 11) the standard recommendation in the PLOS Medicine 2025 study?
In Thailand, seroprevalence rises rapidly during primary school. Targeting 11-year-olds provides optimal timing: high baseline seropositivity (which maximizes TAK-003 efficacy across all 4 serotypes) while preceding the highest clinical disease burden during secondary school and adolescence.
What is the difference between Societal and Healthcare Payer perspectives?
The Societal Perspective encompasses direct medical costs (hospitalization, clinics), direct non-medical costs (transport, caregiver expenses), and indirect costs (lost wages, productivity loss). The Healthcare Payer Perspective accounts strictly for direct medical expenditures reimbursed by national health funds.
Can I export the simulation trajectories for external analysis or reporting?
Yes! Clicking Export CSV in the top header downloads a full multi-year matrix of cases, hospitalizations, DALYs, and economic costs. Export JSON exports structured configuration metadata and aggregate outcomes for exact computational reproducibility.
